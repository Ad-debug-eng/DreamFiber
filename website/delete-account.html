Account & App Data Deletion

Dream Cable and Internet Services Private Limited (DreamFiber)

Subscribers of the DreamFiber mobile application and customer portal (pay.dreamfiber.in) may request permanent erasure of their app profile, cached credentials, location verification telemetry, and push notification tokens at any time using the form below or inside the app under Settings → Privacy & Data Settings.

What is deleted vs. retained:
  • Deleted: App telemetry logs, recorded GPS coordinates, Wi-Fi diagnostic identifiers, device metadata, and Firebase push notification tokens (typically immediately for verified sessions or within 7 business days).
  • Retained by Law: Statutory broadband subscriber KYC, tax invoices, and Department of Telecommunications (DoT) / TRAI mandated IP logs must be retained for the legally prescribed duration under Indian telecom regulations before scheduled deletion.
