Enterprise Infrastructure

What is an Internet Leased Line (ILL)?

An Internet Leased Line (ILL) is a private, dedicated fiber optic circuit linking your facility directly to our core network and Tier-1 upstream transit providers. Unlike shared commercial broadband, 100% of the pipe capacity is reserved exclusively for your organization.

Whether running ERP systems, automated manufacturing robotics in Chakan MIDC, multi-branch MPLS, or enterprise cloud data centers, our ILL ensures you never experience packet drop, throttling, or peak-hour jitter.

The Leased Line Advantage

  • Zero Contention: Guaranteed speed 24/7 without sharing with other businesses.
  • Symmetrical Duplex: Equal upload and download bandwidth for seamless cloud data sync.
  • Multiple Static IPs: /29 and /28 IP blocks allocated for secure firewall and server hosting.
  • Financial SLA: Contractual uptime backed by guaranteed service credit penalties.
  • 4-Hour MTTR: Rapid on-ground technician dispatch anywhere in Chakan & MIDC.
