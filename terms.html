1. Scope of Services

DreamFiber provides high-speed optical fiber broadband, FTTH (Fiber-to-the-Home), internet leased lines (ILL), IPTV live television channels, and bundled over-the-top (OTT) digital content across Chakan, Pune, and neighboring operational zones in Maharashtra, India.

2. Subscriber Identity Verification & KYC Compliance

In accordance with statutory guidelines issued by the Department of Telecommunications (DoT), Government of India, and the Telecom Regulatory Authority of India (TRAI):

  • Subscribers must complete customer verification by furnishing valid Government-approved Proof of Identity (PoI) and Proof of Address (PoA) (such as Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, Passport, or Electricity Bill).
  • DreamFiber reserves the right to withhold service activation or suspend existing services if subscriber details are found incomplete, fraudulent, or unverifiable.

3. Installation & Customer Premises Equipment (CPE)

  • Installation Feasibility: Service provisioning is contingent upon optical fiber technical feasibility at the subscriber's location.
  • Equipment Ownership: Optical Network Terminals (ONT/ONU) or Wi-Fi routers provided by DreamFiber remain the legal property of Dream Cable and Internet Services Private Limited unless explicitly sold under an outright purchase invoice.
  • Care & Return: Subscribers must maintain equipment in good working order. Upon disconnection or service termination, all company-provided equipment must be returned in undamaged condition.

4. Subscription Billing, Validity & Payment Currency

  • Currency of Transaction: All plan charges, subscription fees, deposits, and service fees listed on our website and customer portal are strictly denominated in Indian Rupees (INR / ₹).
  • Prepaid Billing: Retail fiber broadband plans operate on a 100% prepaid model. Service periods correspond to the selected billing cycle (1 Month, 3 Months, 6 Months, or 12 Months).
  • Applicable Taxes: Invoices are subject to statutory Goods and Services Tax (GST 18%) as mandated by the Government of India. Official tax invoices are downloadable through the self-care portal.
  • Payment Gateway: Online renewals and bill payments are routed securely through authorized, PCI-DSS compliant Indian payment gateways. DreamFiber does not store sensitive cardholder CVVs or net banking credentials.

5. Fair Usage Policy (FUP) & Acceptable Use

DreamFiber broadband plans are designed for legitimate residential or enterprise internet connectivity. To prevent network congestion and protect fair quality of service:

  • Standard residential plans are subject to generous Fair Usage limits (3,300 GB / month). Post consumption of high-speed quota, bandwidth speed adjusts to 2 Mbps until the next renewal cycle.
  • Subscribers agree not to use services for unauthorized commercial reselling, public Wi-Fi distribution without proper licensing, host illegal content, distribute malware, or conduct cyber attacks.
  • Subscribers must comply with all provisions of the Indian Information Technology Act, 2000, and amendments thereof.

6. Service Level Agreement (SLA) & Maintenance

DreamFiber strives to maintain a minimum of 99% network uptime for retail fiber connections and 99.5% uptime for dedicated Leased Lines. Scheduled network maintenance will be announced in advance where feasible. Emergency repairs resulting from third-party fiber cuts, road construction, power outages, or natural phenomena will be attended to promptly by our local field technical teams.

7. Suspension & Termination of Service

  • DreamFiber may temporarily suspend or terminate service without prior notice in cases of non-payment upon plan expiry, breach of Acceptable Use Policy, or orders from judicial or governmental authorities.
  • Subscribers may request voluntary cancellation by notifying our support helpline or submitting a written request. Cancellation terms and security deposit returns are governed by our Refunds & Cancellations Policy.
